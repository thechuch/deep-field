The pour-cost math, on Daybreak

A 750ml bottle gives five pours at five ounces. At $16 wholesale, the glass price decides whether it clears her target. Know this before she asks.

Glass priceRevenue per bottlePour costClears 25%?
$16$8020.0%Yes, comfortably
$14$7022.9%Yes
$12$6026.7%No

So: at $16 wholesale you need $14 a glass to clear a 25 percent pour cost. If she wants to pour at $12, say so out loud and propose $14. Do not discount to make her math work.
