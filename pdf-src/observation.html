One account call, watched start to finish. Score what the rep did, not what they know. The course can teach these five and can tell you who has practiced them. It cannot tell you whether they hold up in a doorway, which is what this page is for. Five behaviors, one visit, two minutes to fill in afterward.

Qualified before pitching
Asked what the list or set is missing before opening the bag. Nothing was poured until they knew what the room needed.
O2
Proposed two wines, and named what was held back
Two, not the whole bag. Said out loud which wine is the visit-two conversation, and why.
O3
Led with the farming in plain words, before any certification
Practice first: dry-farmed, sheep in the rows, native yeast, and what it does to the wine. The marks came later, as proof, or not at all.
O4
Answered a money question with a number, and did not discount
Pour cost or margin worked out loud, in the buyer's units. Held the price and offered a return instead of a reduction.
O5
Left with a concrete next step that has a date on it
A staff pour, a tasting, a callback, booked for a named day. Not "I will check back."
