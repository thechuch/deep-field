What we are not claiming

This is not a certification. A scored exam measures decisions made in a chair, one step short of decisions made in a doorway. A pass says the rep can choose the right move when the options are in front of them. It does not say the placement will survive its first menu cycle.

The pitch comparison is self-assessment. The rep scores their own opening line against the same five criteria before and after. We report it as self-assessment and never as a grade, because nobody independently rated it.

No pilot has been run. This course has not yet been through a group of reps in the field, so there is no first-attempt data, no reorder data, and no control group. Any figure on this page describes the design, not its effect. When a pilot runs, the result belongs here, including the part that does not flatter us.

Training is not the whole gap. The failure this course targets, a placement that dies between the yes and the first reorder, is only partly a knowledge and procedure problem. The rest sits in compensation design, route and CRM logic, and whether a manager ever asks about a reorder. We are not claiming to fix those.
